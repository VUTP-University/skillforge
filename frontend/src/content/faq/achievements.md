# Achievements

*Some progress you track. Some progress finds you.*

Achievements are unlocked automatically as you use SkillForge — no separate quest to accept, no button to press. Clear a job, patch a process, finish a Test Suite run, hit a milestone: the system checks in the background, and if you've earned one, it's yours.

---

## How They Work

Every achievement has a single condition attached to it — a completion count, a perfect score, a level threshold. SkillForge checks these conditions right after the actions that could satisfy them: submitting a job, resolving a Stack Trace process, finishing a Test Suite run, uploading an avatar, posting a comment. Meet the condition, and the achievement unlocks immediately. There's no separate claim step, and no way to lose one once it's yours.

Achievements are separate from XP. Earning one doesn't move your Level or Rank — XP still comes only from clearing jobs, resolving processes, and completing Test Suite runs. Achievements are a record of *what* you did, not another way to earn it.

## Categories

Achievements are grouped into four categories, color-coded on your Profile:

| Category | Tied to |
|---|---|
| **Job** | Job Board completions — counts, languages, difficulty |
| **Stack Trace** | Resolved processes, including severity-specific challenges |
| **Test Suite** | Completed runs and perfect scores |
| **General** | Everything else — level, avatar, authored jobs, comments |

## Viewing Your Achievements

Every achievement you've unlocked appears on your **Profile**, just below your details — a grid of cards, each tinted by category, showing its name, description, and the date you earned it. Visit anyone's public profile to see theirs, too.

> *A level tells you how much you've done. An achievement tells you what, specifically, you were doing when it happened.*
