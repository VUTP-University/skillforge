# XP, Levels & Ranks

*Power is not bestowed — it is earned, one trial at a time.*

Experience Points — **XP** — are the measure of your growth in SkillForge. Every quest you conquer adds to your legend. As your XP grows, so too does your **Level** and your **Rank** — the title by which you are known throughout the realm.

---

## Earning XP

- Completing a quest for the **first time** awards XP
- Completing an **Underworld challenge** for the first time also awards XP
- XP is awarded **only once per challenge** — returning to a completed challenge does not grant additional XP
- The more you explore, the more you grow

## Levels

Every **100 XP** you accumulate advances you one level. Your current level and XP progress are always visible on the **Dashboard** and your **Profile**.

There is no ceiling on how high you may rise.

## The Hierarchy of Ranks

As you reach certain levels, your rank — the title by which you are known in the realm — shall change. Each rank represents a new chapter of your journey.

| Rank | Level Requirement |
|------|-------------------|
| Novice | Starting rank |
| Apprentice | Level 5 |
| Journeyman | Level 10 |
| Expert | Level 20 |
| Master | Level 35 |
| Grand Master | Level 50 |

## Viewing Your Progress

Your progress is displayed on the **Dashboard**, where two sacred measures track your growth:

- **XP Progress** — How far you are within your current level, and how close you stand to the next
- **Completion Rate** — The share of all available quests that you have conquered

Your **Profile** page also displays your level, rank, total XP, and the full chronicle of your completions for any who wish to witness your journey.
