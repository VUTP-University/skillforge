# Welcome to SkillForge

*A realm where code is the weapon, knowledge is the armor, and every challenge is a battle worth fighting.*

SkillForge is a kingdom built for those who seek mastery over the ancient arts of programming. Within these halls, adventurers like yourself take on quests, earn Experience Points, and rise through the ranks — from humble Novice to the legendary Grand Master.

---

## What This Realm Offers

SkillForge is not merely a training ground. It is a living world with many paths to walk:

- **Quests** — Programming challenges across four great language kingdoms, each designed to sharpen your mind and refine your craft
- **The Underworld** — A shadowed realm of harder, more fearsome challenges, reserved for those who have already proven themselves
- **The Hall of Legends** — Where the mightiest adventurers are ranked for all of history to see
- **The Guild** — A fellowship of all who walk these halls; explore the profiles of fellow adventurers

## How to Begin

Your journey starts the moment you register. Enter your name, choose your credentials, and the gates of SkillForge shall open before you.

From the **Dashboard**, you may choose your path: select a language kingdom, choose a quest, and begin your trial.

Every completed quest earns you **Experience Points (XP)**, which advance your level and your rank. The further you travel, the greater the challenges — and the greater the glory.

## A Word Before You Proceed

The scrolls gathered in this Codex answer the questions most often brought to these halls by new arrivals. Read them at your leisure, or seek out the tome whose title calls to you.

*All that remains is for you to take the first step.*
